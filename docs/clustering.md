# UrbanTransit IQ — Unsupervised Clustering & Segmentation
- Route Clustering: K-Means with Silhouette scoring and Elbow curve across demand, delay variance, and punctuality.
- Descriptive naming: *High Demand / High Congestion*, *Suburban Feeder / High Reliability*.
- Passenger Segmentation: Behavioral clustering based on trip frequency, peak ratio, and zone diversity.
